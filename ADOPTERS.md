# Adopters

Public list of people and orgs using Witnis (and optionally Harnis) in real workflows.

**Empty by design until someone opts in.** Do not invent logos or customers.

## How to be listed

1. Open a PR adding one row to the table below, **or**  
2. Start a [Show and tell](https://github.com/L00PLeGeNd/Witnis/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) Discussion and ask maintainers to copy you here.

Include only what you are comfortable making public. No requirement to share private repo URLs.
